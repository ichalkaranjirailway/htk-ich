About This Railway

The Hatkanangale-Ichalkaranji railway line is a 191 km proposed railway route that will provide direct rail connectivity to Ichalkaranji city in Maharashtra.

Why This Railway?

  • Ichalkaranji is an industrial city without railway facility
  • Transportation costs are very high
  • Will create employment opportunities
  • Will boost economic development
